cannot certify today's twelve-reading manual-entry curriculum. This additive
local-supervised scenario discovers the actual membership/actions scenes, starts
each through its native Start button, and lets the natural word-count narration
and animations finish. No artificial completion, accelerated clock or forced
click is used. Guide reading writes stay under an ephemeral QA key.

Scope: EN normal motion, desktop1280 and phone390; membership Add and Move,
and source-member Actions. It samples real animation frames, not source-string
presence: visible cursor, compact menu, two illustrated checked boxes, outbound
pointer/ghost relative drift, drop overlay only during overlap and beneath the
ghost, preserved versus removed source membership and final recap/cleanup.

illustrations are not native Moodle transfer commands.
